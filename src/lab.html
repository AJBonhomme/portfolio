Racetrack Lab

Draw a track. A Python controller drives it. · Experimental

Drag your mouse (or finger) on the canvas to sketch a closed loop. The sketch becomes a cone-lined track, and a car running a real Python path-following controller — the same kinematic bicycle model, heading-error and lateral-error feedback I built for Formula Electric at Berkeley — tries to lap it as fast as it can. The Python runs right in your browser via Pyodide; edit it below and hit Apply.
